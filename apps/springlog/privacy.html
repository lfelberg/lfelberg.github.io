Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Springlog collects information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and workout data. We also collect usage information about how you interact with the app, including spring configurations, class history, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve the Springlog service, personalize your workout tracking experience, generate progress analytics, and communicate with you about product updates. Your data helps us understand how students use spring tracking across different studios and equipment brands.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the app, or when required by law. With your permission, workout data can be shared with your instructor through our Instructor Sync feature.

4. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete your account information at any time through the app settings. If you have questions about your data, contact us at privacy@springlog.app.
