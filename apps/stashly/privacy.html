Privacy Policy

Last updated September 3, 2026

Stashly is designed to help you organize your sewing materials while respecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app may handle and how it is used.

Information you provide

Stashly may store details you add about fabrics, patterns, notions, projects, photos, and preferences. This information is used only to provide the app’s organization and planning features.

How information is used

Your information is used to operate, maintain, and improve Stashly. We do not sell your personal information or use your sewing-library content for advertising.

Data storage

Information may be stored locally on your device and, when supported and enabled, synchronized through services provided by your device platform. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Photos and files

Stashly accesses photos or files only when you choose to add them to your library. The app does not access unrelated items in your photo library or file storage.

Analytics and diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostics may be collected to understand crashes, reliability, and general feature usage. This information is used to improve the app and is not intended to identify you.

Data retention and deletion

Your Stashly content remains available while you use the app. You can remove individual records from within the app. Deleting the app may remove locally stored information, subject to any device backups or synchronization services you use.

Children’s privacy

Stashly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Stashly evolves. The latest version will always be published on this page with its effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your information, please contact us.
