No account required

You do not need to create an account, provide an email, or give us your name to use pouchDown. The app works on your device without connecting to a pouchDown account system.

Your data stays on your device

Your logs, cravings, streaks, and progress information are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not upload, sync, or back up this data to our servers. If you delete the app, that local data is removed.

We don't sell or share your data

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Because most data is stored locally, we do not have access to it in the first place.

We may use Apple-provided crash reporting or anonymous analytics to understand app stability and usage trends. These tools do not identify you personally and do not access your pouch logs or journal entries.

App Store purchases

Transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Apple shares limited transaction data with us according to their privacy policies.

Children's privacy

We do not collect information from anyone, including children.
