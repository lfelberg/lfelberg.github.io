Frequently asked questions

Do I have to quit cold turkey?

No. pouchDown is built around gradual tapering. You set a baseline and the app reduces your daily limit over time, which research shows can be more sustainable than quitting abruptly.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your logs and journal stay on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not sell or share your information.

Can I use it for other nicotine products?

pouchDown is specifically designed for nicotine pouches. The same tapering principles can be adapted, but the schedules and metrics are pouch-focused.
