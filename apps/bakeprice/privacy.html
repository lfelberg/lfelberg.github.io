Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account, provide an email, or give us your name to use BakePrice. The app works on your device without connecting to a BakePrice account system.

2. How we use your data

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Because most data is stored locally, we do not have access to it in the first place.

3. App Store purchases

Transactions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Apple shares limited transaction data with us according to their privacy policies.

4. Analytics

We may use Apple-provided crash reporting or anonymous analytics to understand app stability and usage trends. These tools do not identify you personally and do not access your recipes or data.
