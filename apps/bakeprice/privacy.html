Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create a BakePrice account, we collect your name, email address, bakery name, and billing information. We also store the recipes, ingredient costs, customer contacts, and invoices you create while using the app.

2. How we use your data

We use your information to provide pricing calculations, generate invoices, send email notifications, and improve BakePrice. We never sell your recipe data or customer lists to third parties.

3. Data security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. Only you and the team members you invite can access your bakery account.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and optional analytics cookies to understand how bakers use BakePrice. You can manage your preferences at any time.

5. Your rights

You can export, update, or delete your account data from your settings at any time. If you need help, contact us at hello@bakeprice.app.
