On our workbench

What we’re baking next.

Eleven ideas we’re exploring to make recipes easier to manage, businesses simpler to run, and every bake more profitable.

01Collaboration

Teams

Invite team members and securely share recipes, ingredient costs, orders, and other bakery information.

02Recipes

Recipe imports

Bring existing recipes into BakePrice from files or other common formats without retyping every ingredient.

03Recipes

Recipe photos

Add photos to recipes so favorites are easy to recognize, organize, and share with the team.

04Feedback

Ratings pop-up

A friendly, well-timed prompt that makes it easy for happy bakers to rate BakePrice.

05Costs

Ingredient price alerts

Get notified when a key ingredient changes price and see which recipes or margins are affected.

06Recipes

Batch scaling & conversions

Scale a recipe to any yield and convert weights, volumes, and temperatures automatically.

07Inventory

Low-stock reminders

Track ingredient quantities and get a heads-up before flour, butter, or packaging runs low.

08Planning

Order calendar

See orders, prep dates, pickup times, and payment deadlines together in one bakery schedule.

09Insights

Profitability reports

Compare bestsellers, margins, and cost trends to understand which products grow the business.

10Pricing

Recipe-specific labor rates

Set different hourly labor rates for individual recipes when specialized or highly detailed work should be priced differently.

11Costs

Waste & scrap allowance

Add a configurable waste percentage to recipes so spills, trimming, testing, and unusable ingredients are included in the true cost.
