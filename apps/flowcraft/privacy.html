Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account, provide an email, or give us your name to use Flowcraft. The app works on your device without connecting to a Flowcraft account system. We may collect usage data to help improve the app experience, including app crashes, feature usage, and device information.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to provide and improve Flowcraft, and respond to your support requests. We do not sell your personal information or your clients' data.

3. Client data

Any client names, notes, or progress information you enter into Flowcraft is stored locally on your device. You can export or delete this information at any time from within the app.

4. Data sharing

We do not share any of your data.

5. Your rights

You can access, update, or delete your account and data at any time by deleting the application. If you need assistance, contact us.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.

If you have any questions, please contact us.
