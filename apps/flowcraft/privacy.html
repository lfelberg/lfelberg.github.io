Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Information we collect

Flowcraft collects information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and lesson plans created within the app. We also collect usage data to help improve the app experience, including app crashes, feature usage, and device information.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to provide and improve Flowcraft, sync your lesson plans across devices, send important service updates, and respond to your support requests. We do not sell your personal information or your clients' data.

3. Client data

Any client names, notes, or progress information you enter into Flowcraft is stored securely and treated as your data. You can export or delete this information at any time from within the app.

4. Data sharing

We share data only with trusted service providers who help us operate Flowcraft (such as cloud storage and analytics providers). All providers are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

You can access, update, or delete your account and data at any time. If you need assistance, contact us at hello@flowcraft.app.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.

If you have any questions, please contact us at hello@flowcraft.app.
