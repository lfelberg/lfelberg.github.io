Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

SoloSuds collects the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name, email address, and business details. We also store the client homes, schedules, checklists, invoices, and messages you enter to run your cleaning business.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to provide the SoloSuds service, keep your schedule and client records available across devices, send important service updates, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information or your clients' information.

3. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely with encrypted connections and industry-standard access controls. We back up data regularly and monitor for unauthorized access.

4. Your choices

You can access, update, or delete your account and data at any time from within the app. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed within a reasonable period.

5. Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at privacy@bubbl.app.
