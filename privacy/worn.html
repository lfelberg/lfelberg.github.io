Privacy Policy — Effective July 7, 2026

Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in Worn is stored locally on your device, unless you explicitly choose to share a recap card with the community (see below). Worn has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect or sell any of your data, and nothing is transmitted anywhere without your explicit action.

Optional community sharing

Worn includes an optional community feed. If — and only if — you tap the explicit “Share to community” action, your recap card (an optional display name, your savings stats, and an image) is published via Apple iCloud (CloudKit) to a public feed that is visible to all Worn users. Nothing is ever shared without that explicit action.

If you would like a recap card you published to be removed from the public feed, contact us using the link below and we will take it down.

Subscriptions

Worn offers optional paid subscriptions. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your name, payment details, or billing information. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, or want to exercise any of the rights described here, please reach out via github.com/lfelberg.
