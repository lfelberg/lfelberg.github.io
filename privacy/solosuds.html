Privacy Policy — Effective July 7, 2026

Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in SoloSuds is stored locally on your device. SoloSuds has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data — we couldn't even if we wanted to, because it never reaches us.

Subscriptions

SoloSuds offers optional paid subscriptions. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your name, payment details, or billing information. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, or want to exercise any of the rights described here, please reach out via github.com/lfelberg.
